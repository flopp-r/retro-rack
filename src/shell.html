Controls

Aim
Drag across the table and the cue points at your cursor. Double-click to aim at a spot.
Fine aim
←→ or AD turn 0.05° per press (holding does nothing more). Hold Shift with them to turn freely.
Power
↑↓ or WS, or drag the bar.
Spin
Drag on the cue ball, or IJKL. O resets.
Shoot
Space or the Shoot button.
Camera
Right-drag (or Alt-drag) to orbit, wheel to zoom, middle-drag to pan, QE to turn.
Views
C cue view, T overhead, F reset.
Touch
One finger aims. Two fingers orbit and pinch to zoom.
Replay
V or the Replay button watches the last shot again, with slow motion. Orbit the camera while it plays.
Practice
R rerack, U undo, B ball in hand. Move balls lets you drag any ball; Save layout keeps it under Trick shots.

8-ball: pot your group, then the 8. A scratch, hitting the wrong ball first, or no ball reaching a rail after contact is a foul and gives your opponent ball in hand.

9-ball: always hit the lowest ball first. Pot the 9 on a legal shot to win.

Reds & yellows (British pub rules): pot your colour, then the black. Fouls are an in-off, missing every ball, hitting the wrong colour or the black first, or potting your opponent's colour. After a foul your opponent gets two visits, and the first is a free ball: they may hit any ball first. An in-off also gives ball in hand behind the baulk line. Potting the black early, or with a foul, loses the frame.

Side spin makes the cue ball squirt slightly off line. The Full and Ghost + line guides allow for it; the others don't.
